# Abyssal Dominion M230 Production Report

M230 makes M229 encounter identity visible in the normal gameplay HUD without increasing encounter population or UI density. The round minimap now has restrained presentation-only danger rings for elite, monster-territory and admiral/boss reads, while high-priority/selected nameplates can show compact rank and formation cues. Ordinary patrols remain visually quiet.

No combat, spawn, navigation, collision, economy, loot or camera values are changed.
