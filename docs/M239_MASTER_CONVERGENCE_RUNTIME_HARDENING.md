# M239 — V3.8 Master Convergence & Runtime Hardening

M239 deliberately stops the recent sequence of increasingly deep Event/Season presentation work and re-centers production on the full V3.8 game bible. The new convergence matrix classifies major source domains as IMPLEMENTED, PARTIAL, FOUNDATION or MISSING and records concrete source evidence and remaining work. This is a game-source/client audit: replica contracts are not counted as a production backend.

## Canonical combat correction

The V3.8 master repeatedly states that ship orientation, broadside angle and visible model cannons are not part of the damage formula and that positioning revolves around range, speed, map awareness, cooldowns, focus and support. M238 enemy AI still required near-90-degree broadside alignment before it would fire. That made facing affect attack timing even though damage itself was angle-independent. M239 removes that firing gate. Enemy AI can still steer toward a broadside-looking pose for presentation, but a valid in-range attack is no longer blocked by facing. Damage, reload and range values are unchanged.

## Production-readiness boundaries

Auth, repository persistence and transport remain explicit foundations. M239 adds capability reporting to those gateways so future UI/dev tooling can distinguish unavailable stubs from real connected services. No login, backend persistence, payment, server or native-device readiness is claimed.

## Priority reset

The next milestones should close underdeveloped V3.8 gameplay pillars before adding more depth to already-strong Events/Seasons: Sustain/Support, Crew/Officers, Wanted/Bounty, Crafting/Salvage, Compass/Cauldron/Special-PvE, Achievements/Collections, Bazaar/Trading, PvP, Social and then production online services. Visual/HUD/usability convergence continues inside each milestone.
