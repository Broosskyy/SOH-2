# M147 P1-P4 Production Foundation

M147 prepares later asset waves without skipping the P0 vertical-slice acceptance gate.

## Wave mapping

- P1: Main Progression Designs 01-10 and Early Game/first-region families. Ten dedicated GLB swap placeholders are present but not live final art.
- P2: Designs 11-30 and Midgame/Guild/PvP/Special-PvE families. Slots and folder are reserved.
- P3: Designs 31-40 and Late/Legendary families. Slots and folder are reserved.
- P4: Designs 41-50 as an implementation subwave of the master concept's P3 Endgame/Hero priority. It is not a new gameplay progression tier.
- P-Event: separate parallel production; it never changes progression numbering.

## Non-ship families

P1 includes Crimson Sea/Drowned Reach admirals, early frigate families, Krakenling and coastal vegetation kits. P2 covers Stormbound/Cursed/Abyssal Frontier, advanced combat roles, Guild siege, harpoons and corrupted nature. P3 covers Sunken/Void/Sovereign Late Game content. P4 reserves Abyssal Dominion Hero/Ultimate assets.

## Technical rules

Ship Mastery 1-10 remains data and composition driven and never multiplies a design into ten GLBs. Geometry belongs in the GLB; wake, foam, smoke, lights, trails, impacts, magic and movement stay in Godot. Island models contain no baked water/reef/foam. Mobile oblique top-down gameplay zoom remains the acceptance basis.
