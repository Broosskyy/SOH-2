# M177 — Bonusmap Boss Runtime

M177 converts the existing final-boss mechanic names into bounded, data-driven local prototype behavior. The executor deliberately composes existing combat primitives and remains replaceable by authoritative server decisions in the online game.

## Device acceptance

Test every telegraph at normal, Reduced Motion and minimum effect intensity. The warning message, boss target frame and world telegraph must remain readable without covering the map center. Verify that a mechanic cannot fire after boss sinking or outside its configured range.

## Authority acceptance

The runtime may preview authored timings and local damage. Production combat decisions, No-Death rewards, completion rewards and inventory/currency mutations remain server-authoritative.
