# M230 — Visible Encounter Readability

M230 turns the M229 encounter semantics into restrained gameplay presentation. Elite, monster-territory and admiral/boss encounters can now carry a subtle danger ring on the round gameplay minimap. Ordinary patrols stay quiet. Existing nameplates reuse their current layout and only append a compact rank/formation read for high-priority or selected encounters.

The danger rings are not weapon range, aggro range, collision, territory authority or navigation blockers. They are presentation only. M213/M214 camera composition, M228 population caps, M229 encounter semantics, Tap-to-Sail and cross-platform world parity remain unchanged.
