# M245 — Special PvE & Oracle/Compass Foundation

Master basis: V3.8 §§417–469. The terms Compass/Cauldron and Tide Essence remain working names.

M245 keeps the existing Bonusmap runtime and adds the authority/presentation bridge required for the future original Abyssal Dominion Oracle/Compass successor. Tide Essence balance, reward resolution, pity progression, duplicate conversion and claims are server authoritative online. The client may present pool categories and cached authoritative snapshots but does not locally roll or grant rewards.

Special-PvE pillars are Bonusmaps, Admiral Hunts, World Bosses, Monster Hunts, Treasure Expeditions, the Oracle/Compass successor, Secret Routes, Abyss Rifts and Seasonal PvE. Existing combat rules remain unchanged: monster profiles are Harpoon-centered; effective support counts; overheal does not; combat is orientation-independent.

No pity threshold, draw cost, reward quantity or probability is invented in M245 because the master leaves those as content/balance/legal values.
