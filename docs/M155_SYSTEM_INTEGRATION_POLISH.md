# M155 — Systems Integration & Content Polish

M155 continues the broad gameplay/content pass without pretending unfinished balance or art is final.

## Delivered

- Silver and Legendary bonus-map progress now participates in Voyage save/export/import, including wave, kills and completions.
- Uncommitted bonus-map entry receipts are deliberately not restored from disk, preventing a save/reload refund duplication path.
- The captain menu exposes a dedicated Bonusmaps page and routes into the existing harbor Bonusmap panel.
- Seven cannon roles now have explicit tactical role profiles, preferred ammunition guidance and a five-tier presentation progression layered over the existing cannon-upgrade economy rather than duplicating it.
- Five clean-room, visual-only GLB loot archetypes were added: coin cache, salvage bundle, tier chest, Admiral vault and repair crate. Tier chest rarity continues to come from wrapper/material logic, so final models remain replaceable.
- Halloween keeps its existing event runtime and gains an eight-step local-preview reward track. No live rewards are granted and no schedule is hard-coded.
- PL51–60 is staged as content preproduction only. The live map cap remains 50 until regions, encounters, trials and reward bands are actually authored.
- Existing RBAC, validation, review and transport-authority boundaries remain intact for admin publishing.

## Authority and production honesty

All new GLBs are clean-room placeholders. They carry no reward, collision, ownership or combat authority. GLOBAL admin publishing remains locked behind the established trusted backend contract. Halloween track claims are local preview markers only.
