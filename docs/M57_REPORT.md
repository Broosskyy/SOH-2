# M57 — Combat Contribution Contract + Asset Production Kickoff

## Progression
The master-design activity vocabulary is now explicit without inventing balance:
valid damage, qualified ammunition use, kill/assist, elite/admiral/boss participation,
bonusmap, event and eligible PvP/PvE activity. Empty shots, AFK fire and invalid-target
consumption are explicitly anti-farm. No weights or unlock thresholds are invented.
The local ledger is telemetry/presentation only and never grants ownership.

## Asset production
A production kickoff manifest defines the first 13 GLB assets and starts with ship_small.
Stable slots are prepared for the PL1–5 NPC archetypes, Haven's Reach, three early island
shapes and the first early monster. Existing gameplay remains on fallback visuals until
the final GLB is supplied.

## UI
Five M56 progression placeholders are upgraded to polished pure-vector, text-free,
replaceable SVGs and are now rendered in the progression track. The visual pixels do not
own gameplay state.

## Boundary
No fake multiplayer/backend authority was introduced. Future authoritative contribution
evaluation belongs to the real backend/combat authority phase.
