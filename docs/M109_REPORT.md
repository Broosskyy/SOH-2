# M109 — PL6–10 Content & Progression Expansion

M109 turns the already-prepared PL6–15 region/ship data into an actual PL6–10 progression loop.

- Existing map sectors at levels 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 are retained and now expose progression contracts.
- NPC kills award normal player XP, map-progress XP and selected-ship Mastery XP.
- Ship Mastery remains separate per playable design and capped at level 10.
- Admirals and elites award more local prototype Mastery XP than normal NPCs.
- Advancing a map level now requires the local map-XP goal plus the current Admiral clear.
- PL10 additionally requires a Pirate Trial completion at port.
- Existing M56 ship reward milestones remain: Main Design 01 at PL6, 02 at PL8, 03 at PL10. Map level creates eligibility; explicit combat/progression milestones award ownership.
- Stormreach and Ember Shoals have expanded semantic NPC fleet definitions and stable production slots.
- HUD now shows PL6–10 XP, Admiral status and PL10 trial readiness after the beginner strip ends.
- No final balance values or live server authority are claimed.
