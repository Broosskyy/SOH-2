# M191 — Coast, Wake & World Readability

Goal: move the sea map another step away from prototype primitives and toward the master gameplay references without inventing production art.

- Coastline dressing is visual-only and sits closer to the island perimeter.
- A subdued shallow-water shelf improves coast/water separation at gameplay zoom.
- Fallback islands get one asymmetric edge mass to break circular silhouettes.
- Player ship wake gains a restrained center turbulence strip tied to actual speed.
- No collision, navigation, combat, economy, reward or progression values are changed.
