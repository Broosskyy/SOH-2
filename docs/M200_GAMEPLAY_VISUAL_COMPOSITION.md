# M200 Gameplay Visual Composition

Visual target: V3.8 visual masters. The sea chart remains dominant. No permanent bottom menu bar and no centered-bottom primary attack button are introduced.

M200 improves scene readability at normal gameplay zoom: projectile lines must be visible without turning into laser beams; impacts must communicate a hit without obscuring the target; loot must be readable in water without becoming HUD; large islands need an identifiable silhouette/anchor while remaining replaceable production placeholders.

All new geometry is visual-only unless it already belonged to authoritative collision. Cross-platform gameplay/visual parity remains locked.
